RELATIONSHIP OVERVIEW - APPLICATION ASSOCIATIONS ONLY
-----------------------------------------------------

SOS_MESSAGES (0..1) ............... SCF_MESSAGES (0..1)
  sos_messages.message_hash APP-matches scf_messages.message_hash when an SOS
  payload is queued or relayed. No SQLite FK or UNIQUE constraint exists on
  sos_messages.message_hash.

CHAT_MESSAGES (0..1) .............. SCF_MESSAGES (0..1)
  chat_messages.message_hash APP-matches scf_messages.message_hash for relay
  envelopes. chat_messages.message_hash is UNIQUE, but is not a SQLite FK.

SOS_MESSAGES (0..1) ............... CONVERSATIONS (0..N)
  conversations.related_sos_message_hash may retain the originating SOS hash.
  No SQLite FK enforces this link.

SOS_MESSAGES (0..1) ............... CHAT_MESSAGES (0..N)
  chat_messages.related_sos_message_hash may retain the originating SOS hash.
  No SQLite FK enforces this link.

PEERS (0..1) ...................... SCF_PEER_STATUSES (0..N)
  scf_peer_statuses.peer_id identifies the delivery target peer in app logic.
  It is not declared as a SQLite FK to peers.id.
