# Code of conduct

Treat contributors and community members with respect. Welcome questions and
different levels of experience, give constructive feedback, and keep discussion
focused on the project.

Harassment, discrimination, threats, personal attacks, and sharing someone
else's private information without permission are unacceptable in project
spaces, including issues, pull requests, and discussions.

Report conduct concerns privately to a project maintainer you trust. If you
need a private contact, open a GitHub issue asking for one and leave out names,
details, and evidence. If the concern involves a maintainer, contact another
maintainer or use GitHub's reporting tools.

Maintainers may remove harmful content, warn participants, or restrict
participation. They should limit access to reports to people who need them to
respond. Retaliation against anyone who reports a concern is unacceptable.
